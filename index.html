About

Engineering student specializing in Networks and Security, I am passionate about designing, securing, and administering IT infrastructures. My interest in cybersecurity has led me to develop strong skills in networks, systems, and enterprise environment protection.

During my training and projects, I have worked on key technologies such as Active Directory, secure network architectures, authentication mechanisms, and monitoring tools. I notably participated in the implementation and securing of an Active Directory service, a central element in identity and access management in modern infrastructures.

Certified Cisco CCNA and Stormshield CSNA, I have a good understanding of network concepts, segmentation, filtering, and defense in depth. I am also interested in emerging areas such as cloud security, automation, and intrusion detection.

Through this portfolio, I share my projects, technical experiments, and academic work, which illustrate my continuous learning approach and interest in cybersecurity applied to professional environments.
